## Reading notes

- Adopt means: implement in the named EXP phase, with the
  stated validation. Adapt means: implement the principle,
  not the mechanism. Investigate means: the phase must
  produce evidence before a decision. Reject means: do not
  carry the idea into NOVA, with the reason stated.
- No row is adopted on the strength of the reference alone:
  every Adopt row either already exists in NOVA production
  (M01, M02, M07, M08, M20, M32) or lands in a phase whose
  gates (EXP-017 benchmarks, differential campaigns, regtest
  acceptance) decide it on NOVA-side evidence.
- The single most valuable negative result is M03: the
  Qaxe fork's float-difficulty judging is the one pattern
  in these codebases that would actively damage NOVA if
  copied, and EXP-036 exists to forbid it.
