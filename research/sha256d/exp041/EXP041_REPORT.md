# EXP-041: The 29 vs 28 operation codegen gap campaign

Phase 002 step 3. Date: 2026-11-15. Host: AMD EPYC 9D25
(4 vCPUs visible; the live mainnet miner and pruned node
ran throughout and were never restarted; autopilot pid
4778 and miner pid 4781 unchanged at the end of the
campaign). Status: COMPLETE. Production code untouched.
Nothing pushed.

## 1. Question

EXP-020 proved the SHA-256 round is closed at its
vocabulary optimum: 28 vector ops per steady-state round
(12 offset ops + 6 combines + 10 adds), with an SMT lower
bound of 28 in the searched vocabulary. The production
AVX-512 kernel emits 29: gcc compiles Maj as two chained
VPTERNLOGD instructions where the proven form is one
(imm 0xE8). EXP-020's candidate P6C-007, which carried
the proven form, benchmarked 1.051x [0.935, 1.182], not
significant, and was not integrated.

EXP-041 asks, on the current production code: can the
production kernels be made to emit the 28-op form, by
source restructuring, by compiler flags, or by an
explicit intrinsic; and does any 28-op variant produce a
VERIFIED improvement under the EXP-017 decision rule
(3+ sessions, paired 65,536-hash blocks, randomised
AB/BA order, covariate-adjusted bootstrap CI entirely
above 0 in every session and pooled, full correctness
gates, fresh regtest block)?

The proven ceiling for closing this gap is about 3.4
percent of round cost (1 op in 29). A 28-op census is an
instruction-count fact, not a rate claim. This report
keeps the two separate throughout.

## 2. Production state, measured first

Census method (census.py): locate the compression
function body via its local symbol in the built library,
disassemble exactly that range with objdump, and count
vector instructions. v_compress16 compiles to a loop
whose body is one round; the steady-state round
(t >= 16) executes every vector ALU instruction in the
body exactly once (the schedule-expansion instructions
sit behind the t >= 16 branch), so the per-round emitted
cost is the body's vector ALU count: offsets (vprold,
vprord, vpsrld) + combines (vpternlogd and any unfused
logic) + adds (vpaddd). Vector moves are counted
separately; they are outside the EXP-020 vocabulary
census but are part of the real cost.

Production libbal_v512.so, v_compress16: 29 vector ALU
ops per steady-state round = 12 offsets + 7 ternlog +
10 adds. The 7 ternlogs are 0x96 x4 (schedule sigma0,
sigma1, Sigma0, Sigma1: gcc already fuses each XOR3 into
one VPTERNLOGD), 0xe4 x1 (Ch, one VPTERNLOGD with a
permuted operand arrangement), and the Maj pair 0x60
then 0x78 on the same operands. Body: 135 instructions,
64 of them vector moves (prologue and epilogue state
and W0 handling plus the in-loop state rotation and W
ring traffic).

Production libbal_v512dual.so contains two compression
bodies:

- v_compress16 (the batch entry bal_vd_batch2 and the
  search remainder path): 29 ops per round, identical
  census to the v512 kernel. The gap is live here.
- v_compress16x2_reg (the DualMask search body): the
  compiled unit is a looped 8-round unit holding 16
  batch-rounds; its totals (192 offsets, 96 ternlog:
  0x96 x64, 0xca x16, 0xe8 x16; 160 adds; 448 ALU) are
  exactly 28 ops per batch-round. The production search
  path already emits the proven form, including Maj as
  a single 0xE8 VPTERNLOGD. The gap never existed here.

So the campaign's target is the single-batch kernel
mining/cpu/bal_avx512_mb.c (and, by parity, the dual
library's v_compress16 copy).

## 3. Variants and builds

Six research copies of mining/cpu/bal_avx512_mb.c,
generated by build_variants.py, differing only in the
v_compress16 Maj expression (plus Ch for tfull), exports
renamed, built with the production restricted flag set:
gcc -O3 -shared -fPIC -mavx512f -mavx512bw, never
-march=native. Two further flag builds of the unchanged
source were made for census only.

- base: unchanged classic expressions (rebuild control).
- alta: Maj_alt_A, (a&b) ^ (c & (a^b)) (Phase 5 proven).
- altb: Maj_alt_B, (a & (b^c)) ^ (b&c) (Phase 5 proven).
- altc: Maj OR form, (a&b) | (c & (a|b)).
- tmaj: Maj as _mm512_ternarylogic_epi32(a, b, c, 0xE8)
  only; Ch classic.
- tfull: Ch 0xCA and Maj 0xE8 intrinsics; the P6C-007
  form rebuilt on the current production source.

## 4. Census results (gate a)

Per steady-state round in v_compress16:

| build | offsets | ternlog by imm | adds | ALU total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| production v512 | 12 | 0x96 x4, 0xe4, 0x60, 0x78 | 10 | 29 |
| e41base | 12 | 0x96 x4, 0xe4, 0x60, 0x78 | 10 | 29 |
| e41alta | 12 | 0x96 x4, 0xe4, 0x60, 0x78 | 10 | 29 |
| e41altb | 12 | 0x96 x4, 0xe4, 0x60, 0x78 | 10 | 29 |
| e41altc | 12 | 0x96 x4, 0xe4, 0xe0, 0xf8 | 10 | 29 |
| e41tmaj | 12 | 0x96 x4, 0xe4, 0xe8 | 10 | 28 |
| e41tfull | 12 | 0x96 x4, 0xca, 0xe8 | 10 | 28 |
| e41base -O2 | 12 | as base | 10 | 29 |
| e41base -O3 -mtune=znver3 | 12 | as base | 10 | 29 |

Findings:

- Approach 1 (source restructuring) FAILS. gcc's ternlog
  synthesis normalises Maj_alt_A and Maj_alt_B to the
  same 0x60 + 0x78 pair as the classic form; the OR form
  becomes a different pair (0xe0 + 0xf8). No classic
  source form reaches one ternlog.
- Approach 2 (flags) FAILS for this purpose. -O2 and
  -O3 -mtune=znver3 both keep the Maj pair and the 29
  total. (The znver3 build does change scheduling: the
  standalone body drops from 64 to 33 vector moves and
  135 to 113 instructions at the same 29 ALU. That is a
  different hypothesis, recorded in section 9, not
  benchmarked here.)
- Approach 3 (intrinsic) SUCCEEDS at the census. tmaj
  and tfull both emit exactly 28 ops per round, 134
  instructions in the body, one fewer than production.
  tfull's explicit Ch (0xca) replaces gcc's 0xe4 at the
  same cost of one op, confirming Ch was never part of
  the gap.
- The rebuild control is exact: e41base's v_compress16
  and v_walk bodies are instruction-identical to
  production modulo addresses (verified by normalised
  disassembly diff: every differing line is a branch or
  call target). The build route reproduces production
  codegen.

## 5. Correctness gates (gate b)

test_exp041_gates.py applies the programme's standard
gate set (extreme_optimization/tests/gates.py) plus the
EXP-020 group accounting checks to all six variants:
108,000 randomised SHA-256d differentials vs hashlib
per variant (648,000 in total), byte-identical; genesis
digest and genesis search; boundary nonces and ranges
including the group edge at 2^32; template variations;
target exact equality accepted and target minus one
rejected; regtest-easy candidate set equal to a Python
recount; miss and early-exit accounting exact (genesis
early exit attempted = 50,016, the 16-group wave
semantics; partial-group backend 100,016 at count
100,003; 16 at count 5). Result: 102 checks PASS, 0
FAIL (RAW_RESULTS/gates.log). Planted-hit search
exactness is covered by the genesis search, target
equality, and early-exit checks.

## 6. Benchmarks (gate c)

Driver exp041_paired_bench.py, statistic the imported
EXP-017 analyser (exp041_analyze.py). Every comparison:
3 sessions x 160 pairs of 65,536-hash blocks at 2
threads, randomised AB/BA order, steal and runnable
covariates per call, attempted == block size asserted
on every timed call. Batch arms are the counted batch
entries over the genesis header at the difficulty-1
target (category D). Search arms are the counted
search entries over a no-hit 65,536-nonce range
(category E scope: the same counted search entries the
production engine dispatches to, called directly).
Sessions ran strictly one at a time. Conditions were
hard: runnable load about 10 to 12 on 4 vCPUs during
the candidate sessions, with the 1-minute load average
falling from about 15 to about 3 across the campaign;
median arm rates 3.3 to 7.6 MH/s (batch) and 5.9 to
13.0 MH/s (search) reflect that contention.

Adjusted pooled log-ratios (candidate over reference;
ratio = exp of the log-ratio):

| comparison | pooled | 95% CI | ratio | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| batch tmaj vs prod v512 (D) | -0.0006 | [-0.106, +0.106] | 0.999x [0.899, 1.112] | not significant |
| batch tfull vs prod v512 (D) | +0.0301 | [-0.079, +0.140] | 1.031x [0.924, 1.150] | not significant |
| search tmaj vs prod v512 (E) | -0.0213 | [-0.123, +0.080] | 0.979x [0.884, 1.083] | not significant |
| search tmaj vs prod v512dual (E) | -0.5900 | [-0.705, -0.475] | 0.554x [0.494, 0.622] | dual significantly faster |

Per-session adjusted log-ratios: tmaj batch +0.0545,
-0.0196, -0.0357 (no session significant); tfull batch
+0.0413, +0.0836, -0.0465 (none significant); tmaj
search vs v512 -0.1291, -0.1407, +0.2100 (the third
session alone is significant, the first two are
negative; the rule requires every session and pooled,
so it fails); tmaj search vs dual -0.6030, -0.5411,
-0.6201 (every session significant in the dual's
favour; the dual's roughly 1.80x advantage over the
single-batch search is consistent with EXP-026's
1.718x over the production single-batch search).

No candidate comparison satisfies the EXP-017 rule.
The 28-op form produces no verified rate improvement
in either scope. This replicates EXP-020's P6C-007
batch result (+0.0493, CI -0.067 to +0.167) on the
current production code, with a second, minimal-diff
variant (tmaj) beside it.

## 7. The control anomaly, investigated

Two controls were run:

- control_batch_self (production v512 batch against
  itself): pooled -0.0018, CI [-0.117, +0.108]; every
  session contains 0. A clean equal-pair null. The
  measurement stack is sound.
- control_batch_rebuild (production v512 batch against
  the e41base rebuild, instruction-identical code):
  the first three sessions pooled -0.1320, CI [-0.243,
  -0.022] (one session, the first, at -0.3263 during
  the steepest load collapse); three later sessions
  measured +0.4946, +0.4732, +0.5489, each with its CI
  entirely above 0; all six pooled +0.1885, CI
  [+0.111, +0.270].

The two libraries were proven instruction-identical in
every function of the batch path (section 4), so there
is no code difference to attribute this to. A follow-up
diagnostic (layout_probe.py, simple medians, not an
EXP-017 measurement) with solo loads and both load
orders swung in both directions (prod 6.19 to 8.23
MH/s, base 6.29 to 7.93 MH/s across runs) and did not
isolate a stable load-order or per-file effect.

Conclusion recorded plainly: on this oversubscribed VM,
two instruction-identical binaries at different link
layouts can pair-measure tens of percent apart in
either direction depending on when the sessions run.
Cross-library comparisons in these conditions carry a
noise floor far above the +0.034 log-unit ceiling of
the Maj gap. The candidate verdicts do not rest on the
control: they rest on three concordant null point
estimates (EXP-020 tfull form, EXP-041 tfull, EXP-041
tmaj) whose pooled intervals all contain 0 and whose
upper bounds (+0.106 to +0.140) sit at or below the
noise floor the control exposed. The honest statement
is: no measurable improvement at the resolution of
this environment, and no path to a verified one here.

## 9. Mechanism (static reasoning; no perf counters)

Hardware perf counters are not available on this VM
(perf_event_open returns EPERM), so this section is
reasoning from the instruction mix and the dependency
structure, stated as such.

- Critical path. The round's serial chain runs through
  T1: S1 costs three rotates and a ternlog, then T1 is
  a three-add chain ((h + S1) + (ch + K)) + w, then
  e = d + T1, and the next round's S1(e) depends on it.
  Maj feeds the parallel branch: T2 = S0 + Maj, then
  a = T1 + T2. The S0 cone (three rotates, a ternlog,
  the Maj cone, one add) is shorter than the T1 chain
  it joins, so the second Maj ternlog sits on a branch
  with slack. Removing it shortens a non-critical
  branch and does not shorten the round's critical
  path. This predicts the measured null.
- Uop view. One op in 29 is 3.4 percent of the
  compress ALU uops, and the loop body also executes
  roughly a dozen vector moves per round (state
  rotation copies, W ring loads and store), diluting
  the whole-body ceiling to about 2.4 percent, before
  loop, threading, and call overheads. Neither bound
  is the binding constraint at 2 threads under
  contention.
- Corroboration. The DualMask body already embodies
  the 28-op form; EXP-026 showed its advantage came
  from dual-batch latency hiding (1.718x over the
  single-batch search), not from the one-op Maj
  saving, which is present in both its ingredient
  comparisons' noise. Op count within this kernel
  family is not the binding term; latency structure
  and memory traffic are.
- Observation for a future experiment (not tested
  here): -mtune=znver3 leaves the ALU census at 29
  but cuts the standalone compress body's vector
  moves from 64 to 33. Whether that scheduling change
  survives inlining into v_walk or moves any rate is
  UNVERIFIED; it is a scheduling hypothesis, not an
  op-count one, and would need its own experiment ID
  and its own EXP-017 campaign.

  -0.0006 [-0.106, +0.106]; search vs v512 -0.0213

  NULL, replicating EXP-020. Not integrated.
- alta, altb, altc: correctness gates PASS, but the

  REJECTED as routes to the 28-op form.

  the single-ternlog Maj: census stays at 29.
  Verdict: REJECTED for this purpose.

The 29 vs 28 question is now closed on measurement,
not on argument: the 28-op form is reachable in one
line, is correct, is production-path sound, and does
not measurably change the rate on this host, exactly
as EXP-020 found. The remaining gap between the
emitted 29 and the proven 28 is real but inert.

## 11. The diff that would integrate tmaj (NOT applied)

For the record only. In mining/cpu/bal_avx512_mb.c,
v_compress16, replace

    __m512i mj = _mm512_xor_si512(
      _mm512_xor_si512(_mm512_and_epi32(a, b),
                       _mm512_and_epi32(a, c)),
      _mm512_and_epi32(b, c));

with

    __m512i mj = _mm512_ternarylogic_epi32(a, b, c, 0xE8);

mirrored in research/sha256d/extreme_optimization/src/
avx512_mb.c, and the same replacement in the
v_compress16 copy inside mining/cpu/bal_avx512_dual.c
(its batch and remainder path). This diff is NOT
applied and is not recommended on performance grounds:
the rate gate failed. If it is ever applied, the
justification would have to be codegen hygiene
(emitted form matches the proven form), taken as an
owner decision with this null result attached, and it
would need the standard post-integration audit.

## 12. Limitations and UNVERIFIED items

- All measurements are single-host (AMD EPYC 9D25)
  under heavy multi-tenant contention. Nothing was
  measured on any other host; the Intel Xeon 8321HC
  (no SHA extensions, AVX-512 present) has not run
  these variants.
- The null is a resolution statement, not a proof
  that the true effect is exactly zero: the
  environment's cross-library noise floor (section 7)
  is larger than the gap's 3.4 percent ceiling, so an
  effect at or below the ceiling cannot be resolved
  here either way. Three concordant null point
  estimates across two campaigns bound it as not
  decision-grade.
- The znver3 move reduction is UNVERIFIED beyond the
  standalone-body census (section 9).
- The mechanism section is static-schedule reasoning;
  no hardware counter evidence exists on this VM.
- Search scope is the counted production search
  entries called directly (the EXP-017/EXP-020
  scope), not the Python ComputeEngine wrapper;
  batch scope is category D. Neither figure is an
  end-to-end (category F) rate.
