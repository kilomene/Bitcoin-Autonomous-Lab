=== C. Quantile anchors for the exponential waiting time ===
Waiting time is exponential with mean mu = E/R (rate lambda = R/E).
CDF F(t) = 1 - exp(-lambda t); quantile t_q = -ln(1-q) / lambda.
q = 0.5   multiplier -ln(1-q) = 0.6931471806   CDF(multiplier x mean) = 0.5000000000  (must equal q)
q = 0.9   multiplier -ln(1-q) = 2.3025850930   CDF(multiplier x mean) = 0.9000000000  (must equal q)
q = 0.95  multiplier -ln(1-q) = 2.9957322736   CDF(multiplier x mean) = 0.9500000000  (must equal q)
q = 0.99  multiplier -ln(1-q) = 4.6051701860   CDF(multiplier x mean) = 0.9900000000  (must equal q)
ln2  = 0.6931471806  -> median = ln2 x mean

=== D. Difficulty form bridge ===
Difficulty D(T) = T1 / T. Exact: E(T) = 2^256 / (T + 1).
Difficulty form: E approx D * 2^32. The two differ by the
factor (T1 + 1) / (2^256 / 2^32) evaluated at the target:
T1 (D=1): exact E = 4295032833.000015, D*2^32 = 4294967296.000000, ratio = 1.0000152590218967
At smaller targets (larger D) the +1 terms vanish relative to T
and the difficulty form's relative error tends to (T1+1)/2^224 - 1
= -1.525879e-05 plus a 1/T term.
